How we got here

Our Story

Ryan and Theresa met on the burn unit, where he was an intern and she was working as a nurse practitioner. What started as a professional connection quickly grew into something more as they spent time together caring for patients and getting to know each other.

✦

Over time, they bonded over their shared love of caffeine, working out, and video games — and eventually, Theresa added a new little addition to her family: her dog, Kiera, who quickly became best friends with Ryan’s dog, Crixus. From puppy playdates and long shifts to lazy days at home with them, their relationship has been built on laughter, support, and plenty of coffee and energy drinks. They’re so excited to celebrate this next chapter with the people they love most.

In case you’re wondering

Questions & Answers

What is the dress code?

Black tie optional. We encourage formal attire to match the elegance of the evening — think floor-length gowns, cocktail dresses, or dark suits and tuxedos. Deep jewel tones — navy, burgundy, emerald — and other elegant winter colors are especially welcome.

Can I bring a plus one?

Due to venue capacity, we are only able to accommodate those guests formally named on the invitation. If you received a plus one, it will be noted on your invitation.

Are children welcome?

While we love your little ones, our wedding will be primarily an adults-only celebration. Children who are invited will be specifically named on the invitation. If your child is not listed on your invitation, we kindly ask that you make other arrangements. Thank you for understanding!

Is there parking at the venue?

Yes! Complimentary self-parking is available at The Merrill Hotel, including both an outdoor lot and covered garage.

What if I have dietary restrictions?

Please list any food allergies or dietary restrictions when you RSVP — you can see the full dinner menu here on the site. Guests requiring dietary accommodations will have an appropriate plated meal prepared for them.

Will the ceremony and reception be indoors or outdoors?

Everything will be held indoors at The Merrill Hotel — ceremony, cocktail hour, and reception all under one roof, so there is no travel between venues and no need to worry about the winter weather.
